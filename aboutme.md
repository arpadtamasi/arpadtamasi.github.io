I'm Árpád Tamási, based in Budapest.

- **Progos** — AI consultancy for organisations: audits, training, strategy.
- **GoSchool** — an AI learning platform running at universities in Hungary,

- **ELTE Doctoral School of Sociology** — doctoral candidate, supervised by
  Dr. Renáta Németh. Member of RC2S2, the computational social science
  research centre at ELTE.

### What I research

What we hand over to AI when we stop thinking something through ourselves —
and what the quality of that handover, and of the text that comes out of it,
actually is.
